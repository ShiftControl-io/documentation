<p style={{fontSize: "large"}}>What you'll see when you authorize ShiftControl in Google Workspace — and exactly what you're approving.</p>

## Overview

ShiftControl manages your Google Workspace with permissions that Google grants and that you approve. There's no agent installed in your tenant and no master credential. You can grant those permissions in one of two ways:

- **The Google Workspace Marketplace app (recommended).** A Super Admin installs the ShiftControl app from the Google Workspace Marketplace for everyone in your organization. Google grants the app's permissions as part of the install. The connection belongs to your organization: ShiftControl connects as one of your Super Admins and switches to another Super Admin if that admin leaves. See [Google Workspace: Marketplace App or OAuth](/using-shiftcontrol/Settings/directories/#google-workspace-marketplace-app-or-oauth).
- **OAuth.** One admin signs in and approves ShiftControl through Google's standard authorization flow. Every API call ShiftControl makes uses tokens issued through the screens described below, and every token is scoped to what you explicitly approved. The connection depends on that admin's authorization.

The screens below show the OAuth flow. The [scope reference](#technical-reference) also covers the Marketplace app and the permissions for Workspace Audit.

Understanding the authorization flow matters because:

- **You're approving real permissions.** Each permission lets ShiftControl do a specific kind of work in your Workspace. You should know what each one is for.
- **You can delegate the approval.** If you're not a Google Workspace Super Admin, you can send a 48-hour link to someone who is. They approve; you continue.
- **Re-authorization is non-destructive.** Running the flow a second time refreshes tokens — it doesn't reset your org or lose any data.

## What You'll See

The authorization flow has two screens. Both come from Google, not ShiftControl.

### 1. Sign in with Google

You start by choosing a Google Workspace administrator account (or signing in with one if you aren't already). The screen is Google's standard sign-in dialog with the ShiftControl logo and name.

![Google sign-in screen showing 'Sign in to ShiftControl' with the ShiftControl logo and a description of what Google will share with ShiftControl](/img/ShiftControl/OAuth/google/google_authorize_profile_info.png)

Click **Continue** to proceed.

### 2. Approve permissions

What happens next depends on whether this is a new authorization or an update to an existing one.

#### First-time authorization

Google shows the full list of permissions ShiftControl is asking for, each with its own checkbox and a **Select all** at the top. Leave **Select all** checked unless you have a specific reason to deny a permission — ShiftControl features that depend on a denied permission will break.

![Google permissions screen for a new authorization, showing the 'Select what ShiftControl can access' list with permissions all checked](/img/ShiftControl/OAuth/google/google_authorize_new_full_scopes.webp)

The permissions you'll see are:

| Permission as Google shows it | What ShiftControl uses it for |
|-------------------------------|-------------------------------|
| View customer related information | Reading your customer record from Google Workspace |
| View audit reports for your Google Workspace domain | Track third-party app authorizations and help identify who made a change that Workspace Audit detected |
| View and manage the provisioning of user schemas on your domain | Writing the **Teams** attribute into a custom Google user-profile schema so it's visible across directory systems |
| View domains related to your customers | Listing the Google Workspace domains in your org for the Directories page |
| View and manage group subscriptions on your domain | Adding and removing members from Google Groups, including nested groups |
| View and manage organization units on your domain | Reading your Workspace's organization-unit structure so ShiftControl can mirror it |
| View and manage Google Workspace licenses for your domain | Assigning, removing, and reporting on Workspace seat licenses |
| View and manage user aliases on your domain | Managing the email aliases attached to each user |
| View and manage the provisioning of groups on your domain | Creating, updating, and deleting Google Groups |
| View and manage the provisioning of users on your domain | Creating, updating, suspending, and deleting users |
| Manage delegated admin roles for your domain | Reading and updating which users hold which Google Workspace admin roles |
| Manage data access permissions for users on your domain | Listing authorized third-party apps and revoking their access when you block them |
| View and manage the settings of a Google Workspace group | Configuring group-level settings — visibility, who can post, and so on |
| View and manage data transfers between users in your organization | Initiating data transfers when offboarding (move Drive, Calendar, etc. from a departing user to their replacement) |
| View your installed application's licensing information | Checking whether your organization has installed the ShiftControl Marketplace app |

<Admonition type="note" title="The screenshot shows an earlier consent screen">
    The current screen can also show permissions to manage data transfers and view the Marketplace app's installation status.
</Admonition>

Click **Continue** to approve. ShiftControl receives the tokens and the integration starts working immediately.

#### Re-authorization

If you've authorized ShiftControl before, Google shortens the screen — instead of re-listing every permission, it shows a summary banner ("ShiftControl already has some access. See the X services that ShiftControl has some access to.") with a "Make sure you trust ShiftControl" section.

![Google permissions screen for re-authorization, showing the 'ShiftControl already has some access' banner and the trust card](/img/ShiftControl/OAuth/google/google_authorize_update_full_scopes.webp)

Click **Continue** to refresh your tokens. Your existing ShiftControl data (users, groups, app assignments, departments) is untouched.

<Admonition type="note" title="When you'll see the longer screen again">
    If ShiftControl adds a new permission between authorizations, the next re-authorization shows the full permission list again so you can review and approve the new one.
</Admonition>

## What ShiftControl Never Asks For

ShiftControl does not request access to message bodies, file contents, or calendar event details. The scopes on this page cover the authorizing admin's sign-in profile and Workspace administration, not the contents of users' mail, files, or calendars.

Scopes ShiftControl will never request include `https://mail.google.com/`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive`, `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events`, or anything else that touches the contents of mail, files, or calendars.

## Delegated Authorization

Not everyone who starts onboarding or connects the Google directory is a Google Workspace Super Admin. The permissions ShiftControl needs require Super Admin consent — but you don't need to be the Super Admin yourself to start the flow.

Delegated authorization lets a non-admin (or an admin without their credentials in front of them) generate a single-use, 48-hour link and send it to a Super Admin who completes the approval on their end.

### How delegation works

<Steps>
    <Step title="Start authorization">
        From **Settings → Directories**, click the Google connector's **⋮ menu → Authenticate**.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Choose Delegate authorization">
        Instead of signing in yourself, select **Delegate this** and enter the email of your Google Workspace Super Admin.
    </Step>
    <Step title="ShiftControl sends a one-time link">
        The Super Admin receives an email with a unique authorization link. It's valid for 48 hours and single-use. They don't need a ShiftControl account — the link drops them directly onto the Google sign-in / consent screens above.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Super Admin approves">
        They sign in, review the permissions, and click **Continue**. ShiftControl exchanges the resulting code for tokens and links them to your ShiftControl org.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Status updates back in ShiftControl">
        Once the Super Admin finishes, the Google connector status flips to **Enabled / Healthy** in your org. The Super Admin doesn't see any of your ShiftControl data — they only approve the authorization.
    </Step>
</Steps>

### Multiple concurrent delegations

You can have multiple delegation links outstanding for the same Google integration. This is intentional — if you sent a link to one admin and they didn't act on it, you can send another to a different admin without invalidating the first. Whichever one is completed first wins; the others lapse harmlessly at 48 hours.

## Re-Authentication

If your Google integration ever goes Unhealthy — Google revoked the token, the refresh window lapsed, credentials rotated — ShiftControl prompts you to re-authenticate.

Re-auth is non-destructive:

- Successful re-auth **resets the integration's health** back to Healthy.
- Your existing ShiftControl data (users, groups, app assignments, departments) is untouched.
- New tokens replace the old ones; the old ones are revoked.
- Re-auth can be delegated the same way the initial authorization can.

## Things to Know

- **Permissions can change between releases.** When ShiftControl adds a feature that needs a new permission, Google may ask your admin to authorize again. Features that depend on the new permission need that approval.
- **You can audit and revoke at Google.** [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions) shows every third-party app that has access, including ShiftControl. Revoking there is one of two ways to disconnect — the other is **Settings → Directories → Disable** in ShiftControl.
- **Authorization links are single-use.** If you abandon a flow and come back later, you need to start a new one. The original link is no longer valid. This is by design — it prevents replay attacks.

## Technical Reference

The [ShiftControl Marketplace listing](https://workspace.google.com/marketplace/app/shiftcontrol/227648260921) lists the scopes in both tables below. The standard OAuth connection requests the scopes in the first table. If an OAuth connection is missing scopes for Workspace Audit, ShiftControl can ask a Super Admin to approve the additional permissions through Google.

### Standard OAuth connection

| Scope | Consent screen label | What ShiftControl uses it for |
|-------|----------------------|-------------------------------|
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/appsmarketplace.license` | View your installed application's licensing information | Confirm that your organization installed the ShiftControl Marketplace app. This does not check users' Workspace seats. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email` | (Shown on Sign in screen, not the permissions list) | Identify the Google account that authorized the connection. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile` | (Shown on Sign in screen, not the permissions list) | Read the name and profile picture of the admin who authorizes the connection. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.reports.audit.readonly` | View audit reports for your Google Workspace domain | Track third-party app authorizations and help identify who made a change that Workspace Audit detected. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.domain.readonly` | View domains related to your customers | Show your Workspace domains in Directories and distinguish members of your organization from external addresses in Workspace Audit. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group` | View and manage the provisioning of groups on your domain | Create, update, or delete Google Groups. Workspace Audit also uses it if you approve a fix that removes group members. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.member` | View and manage group subscriptions on your domain | See who belongs to Google Groups and add or remove users or nested groups. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.orgunit` | View and manage organization units on your domain | Show and manage the organizational units you use to organize users in Google Workspace. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.rolemanagement` | Manage delegated admin roles for your domain | Show which users hold Google Workspace admin roles and let you update those assignments. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user` | View and manage the provisioning of users on your domain | Create accounts during onboarding and update, suspend, or delete them as needed. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.alias` | View and manage user aliases on your domain | Show, add, or remove alternate email addresses for users. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.security` | Manage data access permissions for users on your domain | Discover which third-party apps users have authorized. [App Blocking](/using-shiftcontrol/Apps/Blocking-apps/) also needs this permission to revoke a blocked app's access. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.userschema` | View and manage the provisioning of user schemas on your domain | Set up custom fields in Google user profiles, such as the field ShiftControl uses to sync team assignments. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.customer.readonly` | View customer related information | Read the customer record that identifies your Google Workspace organization. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/apps.groups.settings` | View and manage the settings of a Google Workspace group | Check and change who can find, join, or post to a group. Workspace Audit checks these settings and changes them only if you approve a supported fix. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/apps.licensing` | View and manage Google Workspace licenses for your domain | Assign or remove Workspace seats and flag suspended accounts that still have a seat. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.datatransfer` | View and manage data transfers between users in your organization | Ask Google to transfer a departing user's data to another user when you offboard them. |

### Additional scopes for Workspace Audit

Workspace Audit uses read-only access to check policies and directory settings. It also uses the domain, Groups Settings, and Google Workspace seat licensing permissions listed above. The Groups Settings permission and the group-management permission can write changes when an authorized admin explicitly applies a supported fix to group settings or removes members. Running checks does not apply fixes.

<div style={{overflowWrap: 'anywhere'}}>

| Scope | What ShiftControl uses it for |
|-------|-------------------------------|
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/cloud-identity.policies.readonly` | Check how your organization applies security policies, such as 2-step verification. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/cloud-identity.inboundsso.readonly` | Check which single sign-on (SSO) profiles apply to your admins. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.readonly` | Find dormant accounts, suspended admins, and users who haven't enrolled in 2-step verification. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.readonly` | Identify which groups to check for risky settings or external members. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.group.member.readonly` | Check for external members and determine which admins inherit SSO settings through group membership. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.orgunit.readonly` | Work out which security and sign-in rules apply to each part of your organization. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.rolemanagement.readonly` | Identify which users hold delegated admin roles so their sign-in settings can be checked. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.user.alias.readonly` | Included in the Workspace Audit permission set. Current checks do not read user aliases. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.directory.userschema.readonly` | Included in the Workspace Audit permission set. Current checks do not read custom profile schemas. |
| `https://www.googleapis.com/auth/admin.datatransfer.readonly` | Included in the Workspace Audit permission set. Current checks do not read data transfer records. |
