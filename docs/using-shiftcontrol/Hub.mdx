## Overview

The **Hub** is ShiftControl's home page. It answers three questions the moment you sign in:

- **How big is your workspace, actually?** Users, installed apps, and discovered apps as live counts.
- **What's worth your attention?** Things ShiftControl has spotted — unused seats, dormant accounts, people without 2-Step Verification, apps holding sensitive access.
- **Where do you go next?** A labelled door into every part of the product, each one saying what it's actually for.

Every number on the page is a link, so anything we flag is one click away from the people or apps behind it.

## How It Works

There's nothing to set up — the Hub is where you land when you sign in, and it's reachable any time from **Home** in the sidebar.

- **Workspace counts** give you the real size of your environment at a glance.
- **What we found** surfaces the things worth knowing before you think to go looking for them: security problems like dormant accounts and missing 2SV, plus cost items like unused seats.
- **Every figure is clickable.** A flagged count takes you straight to the filtered list behind it — for example, the dormant accounts view in [User Management](/using-shiftcontrol/Users/User-management/) — instead of leaving you to rebuild the query yourself.
- **Cards for admins link through too.** Rows that name people — like your admins — open that person's record directly.

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario: A Monday-morning security sweep

Open the Hub, scan **What we found** for dormant accounts and users without 2-Step Verification. Each count links to the exact list of people, so you can work through them one by one — no report to build first.

### Scenario: Chasing down wasted spend

The Hub flags apps with unused seats. Click through to the affected apps, check their [subscription details](/using-shiftcontrol/Apps/Viewing-an-app/), and right-size the licence at renewal instead of discovering the waste on the invoice.

## Things to Know

- **Freshness is visible.** Each card shows when it was last updated, and a refresh button lets you pull the latest numbers on demand.
- **It's a starting point, not a report.** For deeper analysis, use the [Dashboards](/using-shiftcontrol/Dashboards/app-permissions/) and [Reports](/using-shiftcontrol/Reports/App-assignment-reports/) sections.
- **This is where the product is heading.** Expect the Hub to surface more — upcoming contract renewals, ways to spend less, and additional security findings — as it grows.
