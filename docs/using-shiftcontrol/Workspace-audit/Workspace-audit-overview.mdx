Workspace Audit helps you find Google Workspace settings worth reviewing and decide what to fix first.

## Overview

Use Workspace Audit to review access, sharing, account security, and other settings in one place. Findings explain what a setting means and what people may notice if you change it.

## How It Works

Workspace Audit checks the information Google makes available with the permissions you approve. Some settings need a manual review in the Google Admin console. Your plan controls how much detail you can see and which features you can use.

### Areas covered

| Area | Examples of what to review |
| --- | --- |
| Groups | Public conversations, who can join, and outside owners or managers |
| Email | Missing or faulty SPF records, missing DMARC records, and unpublished Google DKIM keys |
| Admins | Admins without 2-Step Verification and suspended or archived users who still hold admin rights |
| Licenses and accounts | Paid licenses for suspended users and shared mailboxes that could use groups |
| Sign-in and passwords | Whether 2-Step Verification is required and whether users have set it up |
| Files and sharing | Public file links, outside sharing, and Google Forms sharing limits |
| Apps and AI | Access to extra Google services and Gemini conversation sharing |
| Meetings, chat and calendar | Outside calendar sharing and warnings before inviting outside guests |

These are areas of review, not a promise that Google exposes every setting for automatic checking. See [Manual checks](/using-shiftcontrol/Workspace-audit/Manual-checks) for settings that need your review.

### Plans and timing

| Feature | Workspace Audit Free | Workspace Audit Standard | Workspace Audit Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduled checks | Every three months | Every hour | Every hour |
| Email alerts | Not included | Included | Included |
| Changes history | Not included | 90 days | Full history |
| Accepted risks | Not included | Not included | Included |
| Compliance view | Not included | Not included | Included |

**ShiftControl Starter** includes Workspace Audit Standard. **ShiftControl Core** and **Enterprise** include Workspace Audit Premium. The details and fix guidance available for an individual finding also depend on your plan.

### Understanding your score

The progress summary shows **checks passed**, a progress bar, and a percentage rounded to a whole number. It shows **Not scored** when no checks count toward the score.

Your score is the share of counted checks that pass:

- Each check counts equally, regardless of severity or how many people or groups it affects.
- Accepted risks leave the score calculation; they do not become passing checks.
- **Manual**, unavailable, and **not in your Google edition** results do not count.
- Severity helps you decide what to fix first. It does not give a check more weight in the score.

### Checks outside your Google edition

Some Google features are available only in particular editions. A finding marked as not in your Google edition is not a failure and does not lower your score.

Missing licensing information is different: Workspace Audit cannot assume that a feature is unavailable just because it cannot read your licenses.

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario: Prioritizing a first review

Start with high-risk findings, open each explanation, and decide whether to fix the setting or review it with the person responsible. Use the score to follow progress, not as a substitute for reading the findings.

### Scenario: Reviewing a setting your edition does not include

Before planning a change, check its edition status. If it is not in your Google edition, it does not count against you. If the result is unavailable instead, review the permissions or access problem.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Open **Workspace Audit** and choose **Findings**.
2. Review the progress summary to understand which checks pass.
3. Open a finding to read what it means and who or what it affects, where that detail is available on your plan.
4. Use [Fixing findings](/using-shiftcontrol/Workspace-audit/Fixing-findings) to choose between a ShiftControl fix and the Google Admin instructions.
5. Review **Needs a look** for settings that need a manual review or could not be checked.

## Things to Know

- A higher score does not certify your organization against a security framework.
- A finding may need more Google permissions before Workspace Audit can check it.
- Scheduled check frequency is separate from email alert frequency.
