Some Google Workspace settings need a person to review them in the Google Admin console. Workspace Audit keeps these separate from checks that passed or failed.

## Overview

Google does not expose every setting to apps. **Manual** means a check needs your review; it does not mean the setting is safe or unsafe.

## How It Works

Use **Needs a look** to review findings that are **Manual** or unavailable.

| Result | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Manual | A person needs to review the setting. | Follow the finding's Google Admin instructions. |
| Unavailable | Workspace Audit could not evaluate the setting, for example because of missing access or a fetch problem. | Review the access or checking problem. Do not treat it as a pass. |

Neither result counts toward your score. An automatically detected failure can also have manual fix instructions: manual remediation is not the same thing as a **Manual** check.

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario: Checking a Calendar connection to Microsoft Exchange

Your organization may need people in Google Calendar to see Exchange availability. Review whether that link is needed and how it connects:

1. Open **Your Google Calendar is linked to Microsoft Exchange**.
2. Sign in with the **Calendar admin** privilege and go to **Apps → Google Workspace → Calendar → Calendar Interop management**.
3. Next to **Exchange availability in Calendar**, open the edit option to see whether the link is on.
4. If nobody needs it, clear **Allow Google Calendar to display Exchange users' availability** and save.
5. If you use Microsoft 365 and need the link, ask your Microsoft 365 admin to configure a **Microsoft 365 (Graph API)** endpoint using the full instructions on the finding.

### Scenario: Reviewing Google Forms sharing limits

You have restricted outside file sharing, but want to check whether Forms follows the same boundary:

1. Open **Google Forms are not covered by your outside sharing limits**.
2. Sign in with the **Drive & Docs admin** privilege.
3. Go to **Apps → Google Workspace → Drive and Docs → Sharing settings** and edit **Form responses**.
4. Select the organizational unit or group to change.
5. Clear the options that allow forms to accept outside responses, or users to submit responses to outside forms, despite your sharing limits.
6. Save. These changes can take up to **24 hours** to apply.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. In **Workspace Audit → Findings**, choose **Needs a look**.
2. Open a finding and check whether its result is **Manual** or unavailable. This tells you whether to review a setting or investigate missing information.
3. Read **How to fix it yourself**, including the required Google admin privilege.
4. Choose **Open in Google Admin** and sign in with an account that has that privilege.
5. Review the current setting and the people it affects before changing it.
6. Follow the finding's steps and save any change you choose to make.

## Things to Know

- A ShiftControl Admin or Fixer role does not itself grant privileges in the Google Admin console.
- Some changes, including the Forms sharing changes above, can take up to 24 hours. Follow the timing stated on the finding rather than expecting every change to be immediate.
- Missing licensing information is not the same as a check being outside your Google edition.
- A manual result is not evidence of a failure, and an unavailable result is not evidence of a pass.
