<p style={{fontSize: "large"}}>The Directory puts people, groups, and app access in one place, so you can find the right person or resource and ask for the access you need.</p>

## Overview

Open **Directory** in the employee portal and choose **People**, **Groups**, or **Apps**. Your organization controls which tabs and request actions are available.

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-people" alt="Directory People tab with searchable employee details" />

## How It Works

### People

Search colleagues by name, email, job title, department, team, location, or manager. Hidden people do not appear to employees, and suspended or archived accounts are excluded.

When JumpCloud is the primary directory, admins can hide people from the employee directory.

### Groups

Browse groups, search by name, and filter by tags. Depending on the organization's settings, you can see group members and request to join or leave a group. Some groups do not show their member list.

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-groups" alt="Directory Groups tab with group tags, the All groups filter, and membership actions" />

#### Show only your groups

The Groups tab lists every group you are allowed to see, which in a larger company can be a long scroll when all you want is to check what you are already in. Switch the dropdown next to the tag filter from **All groups** to **My groups** to list only the groups you belong to.

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-my-groups" alt="Directory Groups tab filtered to My groups, showing only groups the employee belongs to" />

- **It combines with search and tags.** Pick **My groups**, then type a name or choose a tag to narrow your own groups further.
- **Pending requests are not membership.** A group you have asked to join (shown as **Add requested**) stays out of **My groups** until the request is approved. A group you have asked to leave still appears until the removal goes through.
- **It resets on each visit.** The Groups tab always opens on **All groups**, so you never miss a group you could join.

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-group-request" alt="Confirmation dialog for requesting to join Customer Support" />

### Apps

Search the app directory and check whether an app is available to you. If requests are enabled, ask for access or ask to remove access assigned directly to you. If access comes from a group, ask your admin about changing that membership instead.

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-apps" alt="Directory Apps tab with available apps and access states" />

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-app-request" alt="App access request dialog with an approver and justification field" />

<ModeScreenshot path="EmployeePortal/Directory-app-actions" alt="App actions menu with a Request removal option" />

App and group requests are tracked in [My Work Items](/employee-guide/employee-portal/my-work-items/), where you can follow the decision and fulfillment steps. To withdraw an open request, choose **Cancel request** from that app or group's actions menu in Directory. See [My Work Items](/employee-guide/employee-portal/my-work-items/) for the request, approval, and assigned-work steps.

## Common Scenarios

### Find the right person to contact

Search the People tab by a colleague's name, team, or manager. Open their record to see available directory details and copy their email address.

### Request an app or group

Find the app or group, submit the request with the context the approver needs, and follow its status in My Work Items.

## Step-by-Step Guide

1. Open **Directory** and choose the tab for **People**, **Groups**, or **Apps**.
2. Search by name or use the available filters to narrow the list.
3. Open a person, group, or app to review its details and available actions.
4. For a request, provide a clear reason so the approver can understand what access you need.
5. Follow the request in **My Work Items** and read any comments or status updates there.

## Things to Know

- Hidden, suspended, and archived people are not listed in the employee directory. In an organization with two directories, the primary directory's visibility rules take precedence.
- Group member lists may be hidden by organization settings.
- App removal requests apply to access assigned directly to you; group-based access needs to be changed through the group.
- You can't request an app you already have, or one that already has an open request or setup task for you. The app shows its current state instead, for example **Approved. Your access is being set up.**
- **App Access Requests** controls the Apps tab and app request actions; **Directory** controls People and Groups. Work Items must also be enabled for request actions to appear. Group requests require Directory and a group configured to accept requests.
